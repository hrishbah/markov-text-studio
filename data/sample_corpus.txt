The quiet forest holds secrets beyond the trees. Mist curls between moss-covered branches, and sunlight filters through leaves in golden patterns. Old paths wind through ferns that whisper with every passing breeze. A stream murmurs softly, carrying stones polished smooth by time. Birdsong echoes through the canopy, while shadows stretch quietly across damp earth. In this woodland, stillness speaks and time moves gently. Every leaf carries a memory of rain. Every stone remembers the river.
